ABOUT US

"Chiseteiru Animation" is a professional animation production studio originating from Turkey. We value the aesthetics of traditional 2D animation and cinematic direction while creating our works.

Turkiye, where our studio is based, is a country where East and West intersect, and where ancient history and diverse cultures coexist. We aim to make the most of this rich and varied environment to create stories that transcend borders. We strive to produce works that quietly yet surely remain in the memory of those who watch them.

One of our most important goals is to build a bridge between the Turkish and Japanese animation industries. While respecting the great legacy that Japanese animation has built over the years, we hope to cultivate, over time, a relationship where creators from both countries can create something together.

What we value most is the essence of "created by humans." While we are always open to new forms of expression and technology, we do not align with the use of tools that replace human hands or emotions. We believe that a work truly gains a soul only when someone's hands move and emotions are poured into it.
